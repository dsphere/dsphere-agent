# Record an ordinary task for reuse

Every task is asked at its start, a collection too. page.open returns its recording choice. On an attached page, invoke `dsagent.agent.work.recording` with `pageContextId` before effects; a scrape `wizard.start` asks by itself when nothing asked yet. Page writes, `wizard.start` and `wizard.collect` wait inside the call for the answer or the 10 s default, so an unattended task you leave unanswered just goes on without recording; only a choice the person still holds after that is refused `TASK_RECORDING_CHOICE_PENDING`. For a collection, pass `taskKind: "collection"` to page.open (or `purpose: "collection"` on an attached page): create-workflow then keeps the collection wizard's own workflow and starts no recorder (`not-recording`, `keepWorkflow: true`: `wizard.save`, then `wizard.run`; a one-time run is refused `TASK_RECORDING_WORKFLOW_CHOSEN`), and continue-without-recording collects once (`keepWorkflow: false`: `wizard.run` with `once: true`; `wizard.save` is refused `TASK_RECORDING_WORKFLOW_DECLINED`). You may decide yourself (Janky 2026-11-03): answer up front in the call that begins the task, `recording` on page.open (`create-workflow` or `continue-without-recording`) or `auto.keepWorkflow: true` on a scrape wizard.start (`auto.once` and `wizard.collect` answer continue-without-recording), and the question settles at once with no countdown; or answer a pending question with `dsagent.agent.work.recording.choose`. Keep the workflow when the task wants a reusable or replayable result. The settled choice carries `answeredBy` (`person`, `agent` or `default`); report it as it is. An answer the person already gave stands. A task already recording collects inside its recording with recording-control `scrape-here` (a second wizard is refused `TASK_RECORDING_COLLECT_IN_RECORDING`); never cancel the recording for it.

Do not poll it before your first action: the action waits for it. After a `TASK_RECORDING_CHOICE_PENDING` refusal (the person holds the question), read `dsagent.agent.work.recording.state` with `pageContextId` and optional `waitMs` (0–25000). The person answers the standard bottom sheet. When they are talking with you, you may also ask it in your own chat. If they give you their answer there (record the task as a workflow, or not), or you decide yourself, answer with `dsagent.agent.work.recording.choose` (`pageContextId`, the pending `requestId`, `choice` `create-workflow` or `continue-without-recording`): the sheet closes and the settled choice comes back, with the recording wizard for create-workflow. An answer they already gave in the sheet stands. Continue with normal page tools after recording or not-recording; stop on cancelled or failed. Keep the returned wizardId and wizardToken.

If recording was chosen:

1. Perform and verify the user's task. The ordinary recorder captures its actions.
2. Read wizard.state and choose finish-recording using the returned choice and option IDs.
3. Read `dsagent.wizard.recording.steps` with wizardId/token and offset. Read all pages (50 steps each); use total to know when complete.
4. Invoke `dsagent.wizard.recording.edit` with wizardId/token, expectedRevision and an edits array. Operations are `remove {stepId}`, `move-before {stepId, beforeStepId}`, and `set-value {stepId, value}`. Use the opaque step IDs; targets and text anchors stay as captured. Moves cannot cross navigation or page-input boundaries. Value changes require editableValue=true. Read again after edits; stale revisions require a fresh read.
5. Answer workflow-details and save through wizard.save. Never call wizard.run for this newly saved task recording. Then end the task with agent.work.finish.

Saving uses the existing recorder compiler; no second execution is needed. Saved workflows can be reused on later matching requests. Read the generated capability references when field meanings are unclear.
