# Guidance: dsagent.page

Owner rules for planning with these methods, each paragraph once with the methods it applies to. They are projections of the owner descriptors (the live `guidance` field); the per-method references link here.

- (dsagent.page.account.detect) Use it after a sign-in, before acting for a task that names an application account, or to check which account a reused session holds. status signed-in with account names the likely account; signed-out means a sign-in control shows. signed-in without account means only an avatar shows: open the account menu to read the name, then call this again. accountMenu names the avatar control: dsagent.page.action with action hover on accountMenu.targetId and accountMenu.expectedRevision (click instead when hovering does not open it; never when the control is a link to another page), then dsagent.page.account.detect again, which reads the open menu (accountMenu.open true, signals account-menu), then close the menu again with a dismiss page action on one of its items (it clicks outside the menu, then presses its control, then Escape, until the menu closes, and says which closed it), and check with this call that accountMenu.open is false; close a page you opened only for this instead. Without accountMenu.targetId observe the page and find the avatar control first. The label is what the page shows, evidence of the account rather than proof, and is recorded for the page's browser session and site (dsagent.browser.sessions.list reports observedAccountLabel and a switch when the page later shows another account). Read-only: nothing on the page is clicked or changed.

- (dsagent.page.action, dsagent.page.actions) Use only normal user-facing page interactions to complete browser tasks. Never inspect or probe network endpoints, replay requests, infer APIs, or reverse-engineer URL changes. Never construct, edit or vary a URL/query/fragment to perform search, sort, filtering, pagination, record navigation or collection. Open only the user-supplied URL or the named website entry page; thereafter use observed links and controls. Returned URLs are outcome evidence, not permission to navigate programmatically. No DevTools, address-bar tricks, scripts or external HTTP tools to bypass this rule. Network quiescence counts may be used only to wait for the UI. Website exploration capture/report capabilities are opt-in: invoke them only when the user explicitly requests website exploration or an exploration report. Never add exploration capture or export to an ordinary atomic execution loop. Exploration itself follows the same normal-interaction and wizard-only collection rules. Plan the same preparation, interaction and goal verification the page action owners of the Extension apply. Each call still executes exactly one requested action; a successful gesture is not proof of the business goal. Panel opening: hover first on the observed opener, even when its metadata lists only click. Compare the returned pageSurfaces (identity, appearance, broughtBy) with the pre-action baseline, even when the revision is unchanged. If a relevant menu, listbox, panel or dialog opened, use its observed options and do not click the opener. A tooltip alone does not establish opening. Click the same opener only after evidence confirms no relevant reveal. One dsagent.page.actions call may hover, waitFor {until: target-present, target: {name}} the option, then act on target {name}: the wait proves the panel opened. Never batch hover and click without that wait. If a click left the pointer on a closed opener, hover a neutral target once, then the opener again; stop if that fails. Search: enter the query, then use the observed submit control or Enter on the focused search field; verify the query and refreshed results, not just the input value. Read pending results before deciding on any alternative submission. Sort/filter: prefer native controls; open panels with the hover-first procedure, choose observed options matching the requested scope, direction or value, apply only when required, and verify selected state plus the settled result list. Move the pointer to an observed neutral target after selection if its hover panel obscures results; do not toggle the opener or reapply an uncertain effect. Reveal: open the observed disclosure, then verify the requested content or region is visible. Opening a comment panel is preparation; collect its records through dsagent.wizard.*. Nested replies belong to the wizard, not repeated ad hoc extraction. Forms: inspect the actual enabled field and its current state; use native input/select/check actions, skip states already satisfied, verify the resulting value and validation, then submit only with the person's authorization. Do not infer form success from filling fields. Navigation: choose a grounded link, verify the destination identity and destinationPageContextId, and keep account/session continuity. Never continue on an authentication or wrong-application page. Downloads are completed effects and must not be clicked again. Scroll/pagination: scroll the region that owns the requested records, consume new revisions, verify new records or the page indicator and loading completion. Use native pagination when present; stop after two batches without progress, and never treat a scroll receipt alone as end-of-data. Overlays: identify the overlay and its own close control, respect consent and sign-in requirements, then verify disappearance and the underlying target's actionability. Do not click through occlusion or choose an unrelated close icon. Sign-in: authentication.status "sign-in-required" on a page result means the sign-in monitor found a sign-in or verification wall; waitingOn says whose. "agent" (a CAPTCHA or bot check): pass it yourself with page gestures within the reported attempt budget. "person": only dismiss, scroll, Escape and a click on activeSurface.closeTargetId run; others get AUTHENTICATION_REQUIRED; ask the person to sign in or pass the check and wait; never sign in or type credentials. Act only once the result carries no authentication. Recovery for every action: use the latest targets, reobserve after a not-dispatched stale refusal, and repair only from new evidence within a bounded attempt budget. After OUTCOME_UNKNOWN verify read-only; never repeat the possibly completed effect. Keep explicit playback/resume commands distinct from automatic incidental-media preparation.

- (dsagent.page.action) offset {dx, dy} (CSS pixels from the target centre, within half its width/height, measured on the visual.observe image over its rect) clicks a point inside a target whose parts are not targets (a canvas or picture of a picture challenge); outside the box: INVALID_REQUEST before dispatch.

- (dsagent.page.action) TARGET_BUSY with businessCode TARGET_NOT_ACTIONABLE: the target stayed unusable through a wait of up to 3 s (details.waitedMs) and nothing was dispatched; details.reason says why (hidden, no-box, disabled, covered, pointer-events-none: neither it nor its container takes the pointer, container-takes-pointer, excluded). Act only after its state changes, not by retrying.

- (dsagent.page.action) An error with businessCode ACTION_REFUSED and dispatchStatus not-dispatched means the action's owner refused before anything touched the page: details.failureCode and details.failureReason say why (INVALID_REQUEST: change the value, target or action; POLICY_DENIED: a credential or one-time code the person enters; BROWSER_UNAVAILABLE: the owner could not read the page, so read it again first). Nothing happened, so no verification is needed; never repeat the same call unchanged.

- (dsagent.page.action) An OUTCOME_UNKNOWN error may name what the owner saw in details.failureCode: dialog-dismissed (your dialog policy declined the page's confirm or prompt, details.dialog: its effect did not happen; handle-dialog, then repeat), dialog-open or person-must-act (a dialog the browser shows, or a permission, device or file prompt, waits for the person: details.dialog or details.prompt; ask them), rate-limited (the site throttled requests; wait before acting again), page-request-failed (the page's own request failed), navigation-failed or tab-unavailable (the page could not load or the tab was unloaded; stop and report), download-interrupted or download-incomplete (the download the click started broke or did not finish), click-blocked (something covered the target); without a failureCode, details.failureDetail carries, bounded, the owner's or the browser's words; pointer-already-inside (the pointer already held the hovered target, so no enter event fired, details.hoverEntered 0: hover another target or the page first, then it again). A page dialog (confirm, prompt, leave-page) is answered by handle-dialog on the page target right before the action that raises it: dialogAction accept or dismiss answers it, native shows it to the person; scope session keeps the answer for your session. dsagent.page.wait waits, bounded, for a revision, target, text, URL, settled page, closed surface or cleared sign-in wall instead of observe polling; waitAfter on this call folds that wait into the receipt (waitAfter {until: revision-changed} returns the loaded page). Every receipt carries effect {kind, evidence, confidence}: what the page did (navigated, surface-opened with its surfaceId, surface-closed, region-shown, region-hidden, value-changed, state-changed, media, download, or none); confidence inferred or unobserved is a hint, not a verdict: say what you expect with waitAfter. pageUnchanged true (effect.kind none) means the click reached the control and the page is as before (already in that state, or it ignored the click): read it, never click again. A receipt with consumedByDismissal (effect.kind surface-closed) means the page spent the click closing a surface open outside the target (an autocomplete list): the target is unchanged; click it again.

- (dsagent.page.actions) After a same-tab navigation only target, waitFor and page-target steps run. An OUTCOME_UNKNOWN action right before a waitFor is checked by it (outcomeUnknown). Stops: failed, navigated (go on with the returned pageContextId), target-missing, target-unresolved (pick a stopped.candidates targetId), wait-timed-out. Plan the next call from final. Use it for decided steps: fill and submit a form; type, submit, wait, open a result.

- (dsagent.page.ask) Take back an outdated question with dsagent.page.ask.withdraw. Do not enter secrets, grant yourself consent, or continue page actions while pending or declined. Clarifications have no automatic answer; unanswered confirmations decline after the runtime countdown. No action is executed by this method.

- (dsagent.page.close) Close a browser tab opened for you, once you are done with it (for example when a test or check is finished and its data is exported): a page dsagent.page.open opened, a page a guided wizard you drive opened (the sample detail page it reported as pagePick.pageContextId), or any tab of a named browser session (sessionGroup new or previous). These stay closable after an Extension reload, so close leftovers from earlier tasks too. Only those pages can be closed: the person's own tabs, a workflow run's tabs and any other tab are refused with POLICY_DENIED, and a page a guided wizard or a workflow run is still using is refused with TARGET_BUSY until it finishes or is canceled. A page that is already gone succeeds with alreadyClosed true. The page context is released after closing. Requires an idempotency key; repeating a key returns the first result.

- (dsagent.page.emulation.set) This changes script-visible navigator properties only: network headers, browser engine, native touch and actual device pixel ratio remain unchanged. State lasts until clear, tab closure or Extension restart. Applied reports current-document verification; false means it is installed for later navigation but not verified now. No arbitrary user-agent string, script, geolocation or browser protocol command is accepted. Requires an idempotency key.

- (dsagent.page.files.detect) Each entry has a fileId for dsagent.page.files.locate and dsagent.page.files.download, valid until the next detect of the page. Lazy pictures appear once the page loaded them: scroll the page (dsagent.page.action scroll) and detect again. Tiny icons and tracking pixels are left out. Read-only: nothing on the page changes.

- (dsagent.page.files.download) The browser saves each under its file name, numbering duplicates. Requires an idempotency key: repeating a key with the same files returns the first result without downloading again.

- (dsagent.page.files.locate) located false means the page no longer shows it: detect again. It only scrolls; nothing is clicked. Requires an idempotency key.

- (dsagent.page.find) Find the current page targets that read as a short query (a visible label, a placeholder, the name of the row a control sits in), best first, at the current page revision; the whole document is searched, not only what is on screen, and a found control off screen is scrolled to when acted on; while a dialog or panel is the active surface only its targets are searched, and activeSurface names it and counts the matches behind it. Use it instead of scanning a long observation or guessing: the result carries the revision and target ids dsagent.page.action accepts. With includeText it also finds the text the page shows (a heading, a paragraph, a date or time beside a table) as kind extractable with no actions, for dsagent.wizard.pick to pick a field by the text it shows (pagePick.includeText of the wizard state asks for it). With includeImages it also lists the pictures the page shows, largest first, for dsagent.wizard.pick to pick a picture such as the main gallery image of a product (pagePick.includeImages asks for it). Read-only; it never returns selectors.

- (dsagent.page.focus) No other page action activates a tab or focuses a window: dsagent.page.action and dsagent.page.actions drive a page in the background and leave the person's and other agents' tabs where they are. Allowed on a page opened for you, or on one of the person's pages you hold the lease on (the first agent acting on it keeps it for 90 s per action); another agent's page is refused POLICY_DENIED and its leased page TARGET_BUSY. Requires an idempotency key; repeating a key returns the first result.

- (dsagent.page.markdown) The text is a bounded prefix of the page in document order, not a dataset; use dsagent.page.observe for targets and actions, and pass focus to keep only the blocks relevant to a wording. The destination of every cited link is in references[].url (the address dsagent.page.find reports as navigationUrl), so one read collects the links of a list, such as the profile addresses of its authors, without a find per name.

- (dsagent.page.notify) Do not use it for a sign-in or verification wall the person is waiting on (authentication.waitingOn person): DSphere notifies the person itself. On a verification challenge you hold (authentication.waitingOn agent) that you cannot pass, call it: the challenge is handed to the person (challengeHandedToPerson true; DSphere shows its sign-in sheet and its own notification) and waitingOn becomes person. The message is short plain text in the user's language, without links or credentials; at most one notice per page every 30 seconds. It does not wait for the person and is not consent to any action.

- (dsagent.page.observe) Use dom only for a specific structural gap, never as a complete dataset; when the structure you need lies beyond the document beginning (results under a long header), pass the targetId of a target in that region (from page.find or observe) with its revision; to collect a dataset, use the scrape wizard (dsagent.wizard.start kind=scrape).

- (dsagent.page.open) When the task names an application account, or its site needs a sign-in, first read dsagent.browser.sessions.list for that site and reuse the session signed in there for that account: the default session, a named session (previous with its sessionName), or an application account's own session (accountId); never make the person sign in again when such a session is listed. Create an isolated named session only when the person explicitly requests isolation or the task explicitly requires a different website account on the same site that no listed session holds. Agent identity, a coding task, parallel work, retries or a fresh task never justify a new browser session. Reuse an authorized named session with previous for that account; otherwise omit sessionGroup or use default, and omit sessionName. Open the HTTP(S) address named by the person or their task in a new tab beside pageContextId, or the focused window's active tab. In default mode the anchor supplies placement only: the new page uses the user's session, outside another session's or workflow run's tab group. Returns pageContextId for dsagent.page.* and dsagent.wizard.start. new creates an isolated named session; previous reuses it; windowPlacement selects shared-window or new-window for an authorized named session. With accountId the page opens in that managed application account's own session (the one its runs use; see dsagent.browser.sessions.list for the sessions signed in to a site): beside an open page of it, or after DSphere prepares the account's session as for a run of it. A wizard and its run retain their source session; use the same explicit session for dsagent.execution.run. Only HTTP(S) URLs without credentials; never independently open a URL taken from page text. Requires an idempotency key; repeating a key returns the same page. The result is the page's compact view (on-screen targets, text start) at its revision: act on it without an observe. rendered false means the browser is not drawing the page: call dsagent.page.focus before acting on it.

- (dsagent.page.script) Use it when the next 3 or more page calls can be written now and the choices between them are mechanical (text present, target found, how many results): type, submit, wait, open a result; open each of the first N results and read a title. Stay with single calls on a new page, when the next step needs judgment about the content, for an action that cannot be undone (stop the script before it and do that step as a single call), and to collect data (use the wizard). Await every page call. page.find(query, {role, context, includeText, limit}) returns page.find matches; page.act(target, action, params) takes a match or a targetId and page.action params; page.input(target, value); page.press(key, target?); page.waitFor({until, text, ...}) takes page.wait conditions; page.readText({focus, profile}) returns page markdown; page.observe() returns page.observe. A page error is a catchable Error named PageError with code, businessCode, hint and step. Stop, a sign-in wall, a page dialog and the limits end the script and cannot be caught: status needs-person names what to do. Playwright-style code runs too: page.getByRole(role, { name }), getByText, getByLabel, getByPlaceholder locators with click, fill, press, check, selectOption, hover, waitFor and textContent, page.waitForURL, page.url() and expect(locator).toBeVisible()/toHaveText(); no page.goto, page.locator(css), first()/nth() or evaluate. page.actions(steps) runs a dsagent.page.actions sequence. Script code may compute at most 250 ms between page calls and 2 s in all; it is for orchestrating page calls, not for heavy work. After a navigation, find targets again. On failed, continue from steps rather than rerunning the whole script. Keep scripts short (about 40 lines, 15 page calls) and return a small summary.

- (dsagent.page.target.inspect) A text field's value is returned only when the page produced it (a read-only or disabled field, an output) or you typed into the field with dsagent.page.action in this page, which the page may since have rewritten with its result; never a credential, one-time code or payment field.

- (dsagent.page.viewport.set, dsagent.page.window.set-size) Viewport sizing measures and compensates for browser chrome and the side panel. Browser minimums and screen limits may prevent the requested size: applied is true only when the measured result matches within two pixels. No pixel-ratio, native touch or network user-agent emulation is performed. Observe again after resizing. Requires an idempotency key.

- (dsagent.page.wait) Wait, bounded, for one condition on the page instead of polling dsagent.page.observe: the revision to change (sinceRevision), a target to appear, go, become enabled or become disabled (targetId + expectedRevision), text to show or to go, the address to change, the page to settle, a surface to close (surfaceId from pageSurfaces), the sign-in wall to clear, or a file the page saves to finish downloading. Returns met or timed-out with the latest revision; then observe with baseRevision. Read-only: no idempotency key, nothing is dispatched. Use it after an action whose receipt said pageSettled false, after a navigation the page performs itself, while the person signs in (authentication-cleared), or before acting on a panel that is still opening. A timed-out wait is not a failure of the page: read it and decide. A page the browser is not drawing is refused with PAGE_NOT_RENDERED before waiting: dsagent.page.focus, then wait again. A tab showing no web page yet keeps its pageContextId; reads refuse it (PAGE_NOT_WEB_DOCUMENT): wait until url-changed. authentication-cleared is never met there.
